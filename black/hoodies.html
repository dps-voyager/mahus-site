04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you order

Pullover or zip

Both possible. A zip runs about ₹60–70 more and is cut once we know how many zip orders there are — say if you want one.

Embroidery or DTF

Both can be provided. Prints are ₹350 a side; embroidery is quoted per design, stitched instead of printed.

Dispatch

420 GSM: about 20 days from order. Need it sooner? 400 GSM ready stock in M, L and XL — 7 to 10 days.

Something lighter? 300 GSM is supplied for milder winters — indoors around 20°C. Chest sizes XS to 7XL, 36 to 56 inches; send yours in inches, not letters.
